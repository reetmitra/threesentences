## Description (max 4000)
Three Sentences is a private journal built on one rule: write exactly three sentences about your day.

No blank page. No essay. No feed. You open the app, write three sentences, and close it. The Submit button unlocks the moment you reach exactly three.

WHY THREE
Most journaling apps ask you to write a lot, so most days you write nothing. Three sentences is small enough to do on your worst day and big enough to make you choose what actually happened.

HOW IT WORKS
• Write three sentences each day. Change them until the day ends (4am by default, or whenever you set it). After that, your words stand.
• Add a quick check-in if you like: how the day felt in one word, and up to three habits you are working on, answered yes or no. Skip any of it.
• Watch a calendar fill in as you write. A ring marks the days you kept all your habits.
• Open Patterns to see your mood and habits over 7, 30 or 90 days. Tap any day to reread it.
• Get a gentle evening nudge, if you want one, at a time you choose.

363, NOT 365
You cannot be perfect, but you can be relentless. Two days of grace are built in. Miss a day and come back without shame. Empty days are not failures, they are days.

A SMALL CIRCLE, IF YOU WANT ONE
Add a few friends you actually know and message them privately. There is no public feed, no likes and no follower counts. You can block or report anyone from any conversation.

OPTIONAL AI REFLECTION
Once you turn it on, you can ask for a reflection across your recent entries: a theme, an observation and one question to sit with. It never rewrites your words. It is off until you opt in, and you can turn it off at any time. When you ask for one, your entries are sent to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, to produce it. AI reflections can be wrong, and the app is not a substitute for professional help; it shows crisis support resources when your entries suggest you may be struggling.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your journal belongs to you. Your entries are encrypted in transit and at rest, are never sold and are never used to train AI. There are no ads. You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings.

For adults 18 and over.
