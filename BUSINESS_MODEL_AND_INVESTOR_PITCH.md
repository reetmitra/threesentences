## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### What We're Building
**Three Sentences** is a private, intent-focused journaling and accountability platform designed for high achievers who want to build unbreakable daily habits without the friction of traditional journaling or the chaos of social comparison.

Instead of writing endless journal entries that become burdensome, users commit to exactly **three sentences per day**—a constraint that forces clarity, removes decision fatigue, and makes consistency sustainable. The platform combines daily reflection with AI-powered pattern recognition and a curated community of like-minded builders to create accountability without surveillance.

### The Core Problem
**Journaling fails because it's too much.**

People start journaling with good intentions: they buy the expensive notebook, they write eloquently, they reflect deeply. After 3-5 days, they hit a wall. The practice becomes a chore rather than a ritual. The barrier to entry (where do I even start?) combines with scope creep (how much should I write?) to create abandonment.

Traditional journaling apps (Day One, Journey, Penzu) attempted to solve this with features—templates, prompts, analytics—but added MORE friction. Meanwhile, social apps (Twitter, BeReal) built community but destroyed privacy and fostered comparison anxiety.

**The insight:** Constraint breeds clarity. Force a user to write exactly three sentences, and something magical happens: they say only what matters. They ship instead of planning. They show up every day instead of sporadically.

### Our Solution
**Three Sentences** removes the decision-making entirely:

- **One input method:** Three sentences. No more, no less.
- **One commitment:** 363 days per year (2 days of grace—because life happens).
- **Five principles:** Constraint → Grind → Truth → Focus → Execute
- **AI mirror:** Patterns across 7, 30, 90 days of entries—not advice, just reflection.
- **Private community:** Find your people based on shared values, not follower counts.

### Why Now? Why Us?
1. **Cultural tailwinds:** Constraint-based design is trending (BeReal, Strava, Monodraw). Users are rejecting infinite-scroll and feature bloat.
2. **AI inflection:** Language models can now surface meaningful patterns from unstructured text—this was impossible 2 years ago.
3. **Creator burnout:** High achievers are fatigued by algorithmic content, comparison metrics, and "personal brand" maintenance. They want authentic reflection, not performance.
4. **Privacy backlash:** Trust in data handling is at historic lows. Users will pay for privacy.

## PROBLEM ANALYSIS

### The Root Cause: Why Journaling Apps Fail

#### User Journey Breakdown
1. **Week 1 (Honeymoon):** Downloads app, reads motivational onboarding, writes detailed entry (600+ words). *Feels amazing.*
2. **Week 2:** Writes 200-word entries. Feels good but getting repetitive.
3. **Week 3:** Opens app, sees blank page, feels decision fatigue. "What should I write about?" Closes app.
4. **Week 4:** Hasn't opened app in 3 days. Now there's friction: "If I write now, am I breaking the streak?" Abandons app entirely.

**Root causes:**
- **Scope anxiety:** No constraint = infinite possibility = paralysis
- **Completion anxiety:** Entries feel unfinished. Users self-edit. Perfectionism kicks in.
- **Decision fatigue:** "What should I write today?" is a daily micro-question that compounds into friction.
- **Lack of accountability:** No one checks if you journal. It's easy to skip.
- **Comparison trap:** Seeing other people's polished entries triggers impostor syndrome.

#### Why Existing Solutions Fail

| Product | Approach | Why It Fails |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Day One** | Journaling + Rich formatting | Too open-ended; users feel pressure to write "well" |
| **Journey** | Journaling + Analytics | Still too much freedom; features add complexity |
| **Penzu** | Community journaling | Social comparison kills authenticity |
| **Twitter** | Micro-journaling (280 chars) | Public performance > reflection |
| **BeReal** | Constraint (2-min photo) | Visual only; doesn't capture thinking/reflection |
| **Notion** | DIY tools | Requires setup; adds friction |

**The pattern:** Every solution adds features to drive engagement, but features add friction. The real unlock is *subtraction*.

## SOLUTION: THE THREE SENTENCES PHILOSOPHY

### Core Mechanic: The Constraint

**Three sentences. No more. No less.**

This is not arbitrary. Here's why exactly three:

1. **Cognitive load is minimized:** Three ideas fit in short-term memory without cognitive overhead
2. **Time commitment is 5-10 minutes:** Sustainable for daily habit formation
3. **Quality >> Quantity:** Constraint forces users to *think before writing*, not write then think
4. **Auditable:** Easy to verify compliance ("did I write 3 sentences today?")
5. **Shareable with friends:** Accountability partners can quickly verify and celebrate streaks

### The 363 Philosophy

**Not 365. Not 52 weeks. 363.**

- **363 = 365 - 2:** Two days of grace per year
- **Psychology:** Perfectionism kills habits. 365 feels like failure if you miss one day. 363 acknowledges: *life happens*
- **Brand:** "You cannot be perfect. But you can be relentless."
- **Result:** Users who miss a day forgive themselves and show up the next day (vs. abandoning entirely)

### Five Core Principles

These principles guide product decisions and are reinforced throughout the app:

| Principle | Meaning | Implementation |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| **The Constraint** | Three sentences. Say only what matters. | Immutable entries; sentence counter |
| **The Grind** | Show up every single day. | Streak counter; 363 goal; daily reminder |
| **The Truth** | Write it. Leave it. Mean it. No edits. | Immutable entries after 5-15 min grace period |
| **Go Ghost** | Your journal is yours alone. Encrypted. Private by default. | End-to-end encryption; no algorithmic feed; no public profiles |
| **Execute** | Stop planning. Start doing. | Reflection focused on shipping, not theorizing |

### Feature Set (MVP, Q4 2026)

#### Core (Included Free)
- **Daily Entry:** Exactly three sentences, no more/less
- **Streak Tracking:** Visual counter (current streak, best streak, days until 363)
- **Immutable Entries:** After 5-15 min grace period, entries can't be edited
- **Offline Support:** Draft entries locally; sync when online
- **Basic Privacy:** Encrypted at rest; RLS on database

#### Elite ($199/year, 2027+)

- **Webhook/API:** Integrate with Slack, Zapier, custom tools

## TECHNICAL DIFFERENTIATION

### Tech Stack (Current)
- **Mobile:** React Native (Expo) + React Navigation
- **Web:** Next.js 16 + Tailwind + Framer Motion
- **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL) + Edge Functions
- **Auth:** Clerk + Apple Sign-In
- **AI:** Claude API (reflection analysis)
- **Media:** Remotion (video marketing)

### Architectural Advantages

1. **Supabase RLS for Privacy**
   - Row-level security: Users can only read/write their own entries
   - Encrypted at rest + connection encrypted
   - No admin visibility into user data

2. **AI on Structured Data**
   - Entries stored as structured JSON (date, sentences, metadata)
   - Efficient pattern extraction without processing raw text repeatedly
   - Cost-effective LLM calls (summarize instead of re-analyze)

3. **Offline-first Mobile**
   - Entries drafted locally; sync when online
   - No loss of work; robust to network failures
   - Encourages writing without fear of losing data

4. **Edge Functions**
   - AI reflection runs on-demand, not real-time
   - Cost control; better latency
   - Potential for privacy-preserving analysis (no data leaves user's region)

### Scaling Considerations

| Milestone | Users | Challenges | Solution |
|-----------|-------|-----------|----------|
| **100K** | Current path fine | N/A | N/A |
| **1M** | Database reads grow | Need read replicas | Supabase read replicas or PostgRES |
| **5M** | LLM inference cost spirals | Can't call Claude for every user | Batch reflection jobs (daily/weekly) |
| **10M+** | Data privacy at scale | Regulatory (GDPR, CCPA) | Privacy engineer; PII masking |

**Planned improvements:**
- Q1 2027: Add PostgreSQL read replicas

- Q3 2027: Privacy compliance framework (GDPR, CCPA, SOC2 audit)

### Defensible Moat

2. **Community:** Switching costs rise as users connect with accountability partners
3. **Integration ecosystem:** Once teams integrate Three Sentences into their workflow, switching cost > switching benefit
4. **Brand:** "Constraint-first" positioning is hard to copy (existing apps are feature-rich by design philosophy)

## APPENDIX: KEY METRICS & DEFINITIONS

### User Metrics
- **DAU (Daily Active User):** Users who open app and write entry on a given day
- **MAU (Monthly Active User):** Users who open app at least once in a month
- **Engagement (DAU/MAU):** Higher is better; 70%+ means habit-forming
- **Activation:** % of signups who write their first entry within 7 days (target: 50%+)
- **Churn:** % of users who stop writing for 7+ consecutive days (target: <4% monthly)
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** Average revenue per user over their lifetime (target: $150+)
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Cost to acquire one paying customer (target: <$20)

### Revenue Metrics
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue):** Projected annual revenue from subscriptions
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue):** ARR / 12
- **Subscription conversion:** % of free users who upgrade to paid (target: 8-15%)
- **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User):** Total revenue / total users (target: $1-3)
- **Gross Margin:** (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue (target: 85%+)
- **CAC Payback Period:** Time to recover customer acquisition cost (target: <6 months)

### Product Metrics
- **Streak:** Consecutive days of writing (target: 363-day challenge)
- **Retention D7, D30:** % of users still active 7/30 days after signup (target: 50%+)
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score):** "How likely are you to recommend Three Sentences?" (target: 50+)
- **Session length:** Average time spent per entry (target: 7-10 min)
- **Entry consistency:** % of users who write every day (target: 70%+ habit formation)

### Marketing Metrics
- **Viral coefficient:** # of new users per existing user who invites them (target: 0.2-0.5)
- **Referral rate:** % of new users who came from a referral (target: 30%+)
- **Organic growth rate:** Monthly growth from word-of-mouth (target: 10-20% monthly)
- **Paid CAC:** Cost to acquire via ads (target: <$15 for sustainable growth)
- **Brand awareness:** Unprompted recall (target: 5% of TAM by end of Year 2)

## APPENDIX: FOUNDER NARRATIVE

### Why I'm Building This

I built Three Sentences because I was failing at journaling.

I'd buy the nice notebook, write eloquently for 3-5 days, then stop. The barrier to entry was too high; the commitment felt vague. I knew reflection was important, but the practice wasn't sticking.

Then I experimented: **What if I forced myself to write exactly three sentences per day?**

Three sentences takes 5-10 minutes. Short enough to be a daily habit, long enough to say something meaningful. And something magical happened: instead of writing *more*, I wrote *better*. The constraint forced me to think before writing. The time commitment was sustainable. The streak counter created accountability without feeling punitive.

Six months later, I'd written 180+ entries. I could see patterns in my own thinking—about shipping, about decision-making, about the moments that actually mattered. And I realized: **I'm not the only person who needs this.**

Every founder, athlete, creator, and builder I know struggles with reflection. They journal sporadically. They avoid it because it feels like a chore. Or they use Slack channels and journals to "reflect" publicly, which destroys authenticity.

So I'm building the anti-app: A journaling platform that removes features, not adds them. That respects privacy. That creates accountability without performance anxiety. That understands that constraint is a feature, not a limitation.

**This is for people who want to get better, but don't want to perform better. For people who build, but take time to reflect. For people who understand that the days you show up matter more than the days you shine.**
